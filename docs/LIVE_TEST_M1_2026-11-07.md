# Live video test on the M1 — 2026-11-07

`venv/bin/python -m race_day.live_test` on the race-day computer (Apple M1, 8 GB).

- **Event:** Race 1 Season 2027. Heats 2, 3, 6 and 9, 4 pilots each, one every 240 s.
- **Live video:** 30 fps a pilot, with the detections kept for the heat analysis.
- **Analysis:** paused while a heat races ("Pause during races").
- **Runs:** with 1 live process, then with 2. Then each heat once more the normal way (no live video, no pause, one after another) to compare.

- **Heat 6 with 2 processes failed.** The live video recorded 1 frame and gave no stats, then worked again for heat 9. There was no error in the log. This is still to be found.

*Real work* for heats 3 and 6 is worked out from the log. The test's own "Worked" column counted paused time while a heat was still waiting in the queue, so it showed too little for those heats.

- **The live detections never changed a result.** The passes were the same in every heat.
- **They save only 5–15% of the analysis.** The live video covers just 20–35% of the frames the analysis uses. It picks frames by time, so about half of them land on frames the analysis skips.
- **With "Pause during races" the M1 falls behind.** A heat needs about 220–330 s of work. A 4-minute cycle leaves only about 90 s outside the 150 s of racing. Each heat adds about 2.5 min of backlog, and by the 4th heat results came 11–12 min after the race.

## What this means for race day on the M1

**Setup: no mid-lap positions, Auto mode, "Pause during races" off.** The normal analysis part of the test is this case. The heats ran back to back, and spacing them 4 min apart only helps:

| Heat | Race ends at | Ready at | Ready after its race |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0:00 | 4:16 | 4:16 |
| 3 | 4:00 | 8:16 | 4:16 |
| 6 | 8:00 | 11:54 | 3:54 |
| 9 | 12:00 | 17:28 | 5:28 |

- Results arrive about one race later and don't pile up. Long heats (300 s and more) add a little delay, and shorter heats catch it up again.
- **Not measured yet:** FPVTrackside recording 4 channels at the same time. A short session of real races (master branch, no mid-lap positions, pause off) will show whether that slows the analysis enough to matter.

**With mid-lap positions:** use 2 live processes. The full analysis can't keep up with "Pause during races" on, so it needs one of these:

- accept a growing delay;
- turn the pause off and test the live video and the analysis running together;
- run the full analysis on a faster computer.
